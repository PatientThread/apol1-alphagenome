Dear Editors,

I am submitting "Reference choice affects positional enrichment of AlphaGenome
variant rankings at APOL1-MYH9" for consideration as a Research note.

The paper reports a single empirical observation. Ranking common variants at the
APOL1-MYH9 locus by a podocyte-labelled AlphaGenome accessibility score, the
apparent strength of positional enrichment, and the interpretation it invites,
changed substantially with the measured ENCODE reference set used to evaluate
it: odds ratio 5.98 against whole-kidney peaks and 8.76 against a
podocyte-inclusive union, over the same variants and the same ranking. A
beta-globin comparison locus behaved the same way, giving 1.44 under one
reference and 7.54 under the other.

The work began as a regulatory-variant prioritisation project at APOL1-MYH9. It
did not produce a candidate list I was willing to report, and I am submitting it
because the reference sensitivity encountered on the way seems more useful than
the candidates would have been. Each reference set is a defensible choice, the
differences between them are easy to overlook, and the paper sets out three
practical safeguards that follow. I hope this fits your remit for informative
projects that did not yield their originally intended result.

The claims are deliberately narrow. The evaluation overlaps the model's training
data and tests position rather than allelic effect, so it does not validate
tissue-specific variant-effect accuracy, does not establish equivalent
performance between the two loci, and does not identify an APOL1 regulatory
modifier. Each of these is stated in the Limitations.

All code and frozen results, including the allele ledger, per-variant
predictions, every contingency table, the ENCODE peak and track manifests, the
bootstrap pseudocode and its stability tables, are archived at Zenodo,
doi:10.5281/zenodo.22875737, and openly available.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and reports no
new participant data, so ethical approval was not required. I am the sole
author, declare no competing interests, and received no funding. In line with
your policy on generative AI, I used Claude (Opus 5, Anthropic) to search and
verify the literature, to check figures and citations against primary sources,
and to draft and revise the text and analysis code; this is declared in the
Acknowledgements, the tool is not listed as an author, and I take full
responsibility for the content.
